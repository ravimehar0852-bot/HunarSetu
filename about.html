About HunarSetu

Local Skills. Local Work. Local Growth.

HunarSetu ek local services platform hai jiska purpose local customers ko skilled workers aur service providers se connect karna hai.

Humari shuruaat Jhalawar, Rajasthan se ho rahi hai, jahan log apne aas-paas ke electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, mechanics, tutors aur doosre service professionals ko aasani se discover kar sakein.

HunarSetu ka idea

Bahut se skilled workers apna kaam achhe se karte hain, lekin unhe naye customers tak pahunchne ke liye online visibility nahi mil pati.

Dusri taraf customers ko bhi apne area mein trusted service provider dhoondhne mein dikkat hoti hai.

HunarSetu in dono ko ek simple digital platform par connect karne ka प्रयास karta hai.

Our Mission

Local talent ko discover karna easy banana aur customers ko nearby services tak simple access dena.

🛠️ Local Opportunity

📍 Local Services

Mobile-friendly aur simple interface jise koi bhi aasani se use kar sake.

How HunarSetu Works

1. Service Choose Karein

Apni required service select karein.

2. Worker Discover Karein

Available local workers ke profiles dekhein.

3. Worker Select Karein

Apni requirement ke according suitable worker choose karein.

4. Service Book Karein

Worker ke saath service booking process complete karein.

Our Vision

HunarSetu ka long-term vision hai ki local skilled workers ko digital economy ka hissa banaya ja sake aur customers ke liye reliable local services ko easily accessible banaya ja sake.

Shuruaat Jhalawar se hai, lekin future mein is model ko doosre cities aur districts tak expand kiya ja sakta hai.

Transparency

HunarSetu ek developing platform hai. Hum kisi government partnership, government approval, ya verified worker network ka claim nahi karte jab tak woh officially established na ho.
