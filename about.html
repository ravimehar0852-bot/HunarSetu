About HunarSetu

Setu means bridge. HunarSetu aims to connect local talent with local customers, starting with Jhalawar, Rajasthan.

Our mission

Make skilled workers easier to discover and help people find nearby skills quickly.

Local opportunity

More visibility for people who work with their hands.

Accessibility

Simple, mobile-first design in plain language.

Transparency

Clear labels on what is demo and what is real.

This is an early prototype. We do not claim partnerships, government approval, or a verified worker network.
